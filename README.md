This repository contains a domestic violence protective order (DVPO) filed against the defendant, Ryan Joseph Farrell, in 2023 in Chatham County, North Carolina (NC). The file contains: (1) the original ex parte order against the defendant, (2) evidence and subjective account of events submitted by the plaintiff (Elizabeth D. Wilson) in case of a trial submitted on 1/10/2023, (3) the granted order from 1/18/2023, and (4) the order to set aside the DVPO from 4/12/2023. 
Note that much of the evidence provided in this file is a subjective account of events submitted by the plaintiff (Elizabeth D. Wilson). Therefore, this subjective evidence submitted by the plaintiff does not constitute objective fact. Notably, the defendant did not dispute this DVPO. Therefore, these pieces of evidence submitted by the plaintiff were not reviewed critically by a judge or jury. Thus, the judge was not forced to decide whether the acts constituted domestic violence. 
Notably, the judge did report the following findings (page 19 of this PDF file): "The defendant has committed acts of domestic violence against the plaintiff" and "It clearly appears that there is a danger of acts of domestic violence against the plaintiff." 
I am not posting this information with malicious intent. I do not intend to defame the plaintiff or cause reputational harm. Rather, I believe it is in the general public's interest to have this information.
